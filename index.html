01 — About

A roastery in a 1920s shophouse.

Brass & Bean started in 2019 with one second-hand drum roaster and a folding table by the door. The building is older than the coffee: a two-storey shophouse with air wells, worn brass fittings and floor tiles we were politely told not to touch.

We still roast twice a week, in batches small enough that every bag is weighed, sealed and dated by hand. Nothing leaves the counter older than ten days.

If we can’t explain where a coffee came from, it doesn’t go on the bar.

  • Roasted here

    Twice a week, on a 5 kg drum roaster at the back of the room.

  • Single origin

    Three farms we buy from directly, year after year.

  • Quiet room

    Wifi, plug points and no background music after eleven.
